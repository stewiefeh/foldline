Every handle is shaped on the same bench as the blade: oiled walnut over a bone liner, pinned in brass. Send a tracing of your grip and we shape to it.

The Forge

Two smiths, one bench.

Foldline is Elias Brandt and Marta Voss. They met at a smithing school in Stockholm, bought a coal forge at auction, and moved it to Duluth. They make knives the slow way because it is the only way they know that lasts.
